# HOL-01 — Exact finite monodromy certificate at p = 7 for a Barning-Berggren congruence graph

[![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.22978389.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22978389)

**Version 1.1.1 — finite computational certificate with an explicit claim boundary**

> **Scope correction in 1.1.1.** The exact p = 7 certificate is unchanged. Version 1.1.1 withdraws the v1.1.0 wording that presented a theorem for every odd prime and an extension "verbatim" to every congruence level. The additional primes and the p^3 -> p^2 checks are now labelled exactly as what they are: finite regression/exploratory computations. Version 1.1.0 was an internal release candidate and was not published.

This repository gives a self-contained, exact certificate for a non-abelian monodromy phenomenon in finite congruence quotients generated by the classical Barning-Berggren matrices for primitive Pythagorean triples.

The principal certified case is

\[
\Gamma_{49}\longrightarrow \Gamma_7
\]

above the root \(r=(3,4,5)^T\). The programs use integer and modular arithmetic only and the Python standard library only.

## Main certified statement at p = 7

Let

\[
Q(x,y,z)=x^2+y^2-z^2
\]

and let \(M_1,M_2,M_3\) be the classical Barning-Berggren matrices. For a modulus \(m\), let \(V_m\) be the orbit of \(r\) modulo \(m\) under the symmetric labelled generating set \(M_i^{\pm1}\).

The exact finite certificate verifies:

1. \(|V_7|=24\) and \(|V_{49}|=1176\).
2. Reduction \(V_{49}\to V_7\) has 49 points in every fibre.
3. If \(T_7=r^\perp\subset \mathbf F_7^3\) for the bilinear form attached to \(Q\), the root fibre is the affine torsor
   \[
   r+7T_7\pmod{49}.
   \]
   Choosing the distinguished lift \(r\) as origin identifies this torsor with \(T_7\); only after choosing a basis of the two-dimensional vector space \(T_7\) do we write coordinates \(\mathbf F_7^2\).
4. The induced monodromy permutation group on the 49-point root fibre has order
   \[
   686=2\cdot7^3.
   \]
5. Exact finite closure verifies a translation kernel of order 49, a dihedral linear image of order 14, and a zero-translation complement of order 14. In chosen affine coordinates the computed group is therefore
   \[
   \mathbf F_7^2\rtimes D_{14},
   \]
   with \(D_{14}\) denoting the dihedral group of order 14.
6. Two explicit positive loops generate the index-two subgroup of order \(343=7^3\), and that subgroup is algebraically identified with \(UT_3(\mathbf F_7)\).

Items 1–5 are finite exact computer-certified statements. Item 6 also has a short algebraic proof from the explicit affine formulas recorded in the note.

## Two explicit loops

Write \(A=M_3\), \(B=M_2\), \(C=M_1\). The positive words

\[
\gamma_1=BABC,\qquad \gamma_2=BABAA
\]

close modulo 7 and lift nontrivially modulo 49. Their actions on the 49-point fibre each have cycle signature \(7^7\), and they do not commute.

In the coordinate basis used by `finite_prime_regression_check.py`, their affine actions are

\[
g_1(s,t)=(s+6t+5,\ t+3),\qquad
g_2(s,t)=(s+2t,\ t+3).
\]

Writing

\[
T(a,b,c):(s,t)\mapsto(s+at+c,t+b),
\]

we have \(g_1=T(6,3,5)\), \(g_2=T(2,3,0)\). The law

\[
T(a,b,c)T(a',b',c')=T(a+a',b+b',c+c'+ab')
\]

is the standard affine realization of the finite Heisenberg group. Since \((6,3)\) and \((2,3)\) are independent in \(\mathbf F_7^2\) and the commutator is the nontrivial central translation \(T(0,0,5)\), the two loops generate all \(7^3\) elements of this group.

## General structural formula that is safe to use

There is one elementary formula used to organise the computations. If a matrix word \(W\) satisfies \(Wr\equiv r\pmod p\), write

\[
\delta_p(W)=\frac{Wr-r}{p}\pmod p.
\]

On a first-order lift \(r+pu\),

\[
W(r+pu)\equiv r+p(\bar W u+\delta_p(W))\pmod{p^2},
\]

so the action is affine. Moreover

\[
\delta_p(W_1W_2)=\delta_p(W_1)+\bar W_1\delta_p(W_2).
\]

This formula is a starting point for a future uniform theorem. This release does **not** identify the image of that affine representation for arbitrary \(p\) or arbitrary congruence level.

## Claim boundary

The authoritative status table is `HOL01_STATUS.md`.

This release:

- **certifies** the finite p = 7 statements above;
- **proves algebraically** the Heisenberg identification for the subgroup generated by `BABC` and `BABAA` from explicit affine formulas;
- reports \(p=3,5,11,13\) only as finite regression checks;
- reports \(p^3\to p^2\) at \(p=3,5,7\) only as exploratory exact checks;
- does **not** claim \(\mathrm{Mon}_p\cong\mathbf F_p^2\rtimes D_{2p}\) for every odd prime;
- does **not** claim the same monodromy at every level \(p^{n+1}\to p^n\);
- does **not** claim a new general theory of graph monodromy, holonomy, curvature, or Pythagorean triples;
- makes no claim concerning RH or Hilbert-Pólya.
