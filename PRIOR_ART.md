# Prior-art and literature-context note

**Search window:** 2026-09-21 to 2026-09-24  
**Release:** v1.1.1

This note documents a targeted literature search. It is not an exhaustive novelty search and is not a legal opinion. Failure to locate an exact prior statement is not evidence that none exists.

## What is plainly classical or already studied

- the Barning/Berggren generation of primitive Pythagorean triples;
- Romik's dynamical formulation and the modular-group background;
- the 2x2/3x3 orthogonal representation behind the Pythagorean parametrisation;
- congruence reduction of Pythagorean triples and related orbit questions;
- graph coverings, Schreier graphs and permutation/voltage descriptions of monodromy;
- Heisenberg groups as standard central extensions;
- finite orthogonal/linear group and congruence-filtration techniques.

## Publication wording adopted for v1.1.1

- **Principal certified fact:** the exact finite p = 7 certificate.
- **Algebraic proof:** the p = 7 Heisenberg identification for the subgroup generated by the two explicit loops.
- **Finite regression evidence:** p = 3, 5, 11, 13 in addition to p = 7.
- **Exploratory finite evidence:** one higher tower step at p = 3, 5, 7.
- **No universal theorem claim:** no assertion that the observed group formula holds for every odd prime or every level.
- **No priority claim:** timestamps and reproducibility are not a novelty determination.

The classical 2x2/3x3 representation and congruence-group language remain useful for a future proof. They are not used in v1.1.1 as a substitute for the missing uniform stabilizer/kernel argument.
