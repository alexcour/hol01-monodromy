# AI assistance disclosure

AI systems were used during parts of the wider research workflow for drafting, code assistance, literature orientation, adversarial checking, and editorial review.

They are **not** listed as authors.

The public mathematical claims in this repository are supported by the cited literature, exact scripts, deterministic snapshots, and the explicit claim boundary in \`HOL01_STATUS.md\`. Responsibility for deciding what is released, what is claimed, and how sources are attributed remains with Alexandre Couret.

AI output is not treated as evidence of mathematical truth or historical priority. When an AI-assisted suggestion affected a public statement, the statement was required to survive independent checking against executable artefacts or external sources before inclusion.

The disclosure is intentionally general: it documents the role of AI assistance without turning private conversations or unreleased research material into part of the public dataset.
